## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** designed to analyze dynamic mobile phone sales performance, customer purchasing behaviors, and regional distribution. This business intelligence solution consolidates transactional data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to optimize inventory management, track revenue growth, and evaluate customer satisfaction trends.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Visualizations
* **Executive KPIs:** Real-time visibility into high-level metrics including Total Sales Revenue, Transaction Volume, Units Sold, and Average Rating.
* **Geographical Distribution:** An interactive map visual highlighting regional sales hot-spots and geographic demand density.
* **Sales & Volume Trends:** Historical line and area charts tracking *Total Quantity by Month* and *Total Sales by Day Name* to identify peak shopping periods.
* **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Granular performance breakdowns showcasing Customer Ratings distribution and *Transactions by Payment Method* (Pie Chart analysis).
* **Dynamic Slicers:** Comprehensive filtering capabilities by time horizons (Months/Days), specific mobile brands, and payment options.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema design establishing relationships between Transaction Fact tables and Dimension tables (Customer, Date, Product, Geography).
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Formulated custom measures for Time-Intelligence reporting, running totals, and dynamic key performance metrics.
* **Data Transformation (Power Query):** Executed advanced ETL processes including data cleaning, data type standardizations, conditional column generation, and structural schema modifications.

---

## 💡 Business Insights & Value Delivered
* **Peak Performance Tracking:** Identified specific days of the week and seasonal months driving the highest sales volume, enabling targeted marketing campaigns.
* **Payment Preference Optimization:** Revealed the preferred transaction methods of customers, allowing the finance team to optimize payment gateway partnerships.
* **Product Health Monitoring:** Correlated lower customer rating clusters with specific brands to highlight potential quality control or supply chain bottlenecks.
